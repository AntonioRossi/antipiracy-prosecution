# Known Issues

## KI-001 — Provisional pagination used by US counsel is not reconciled with the local as-filed copy

**Status:** Open — the source copy or pagination convention used by US counsel has not been identified.

**Affected records:**

- `from-US-Counsel/260809/260809-email-da-alessandro.md`
- `from-US-Counsel/260804/AA11393US-manifest-matching-claim-language_MEMO.md`
- `PPA2/as filed 63 557868.pdf`

**Issue:** The 9 August US-counsel email identifies the direction-neutral metacode at provisional “document pages 30-31” and the segmented-Tardos disclosure at “document pages 44-45.” Those page references do not correspond to the local as-filed provisional PDF declared and cited in the applicant memorandum.

**Verified local mapping:**

| Disclosure | Printed specification pages | PDF pages in `PPA2/as filed 63 557868.pdf` |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Direction-neutral metacode, including the `1 : -1` variation | 14–15 | 25–26 |
| Collusion and segmented-Tardos discussion | 27–28 | 38–39 |
| Express “Segmented Tardos Codes” passage | 28 | 39 |

The local as-filed PDF contains 44 pages, so counsel's reference to document page 45 necessarily uses a different copy, a different document assembly, or a different pagination convention.

**Current impact:** The discrepancy does not affect the counsel-final claims or abstract. It does not affect the counsel-final claim-support statement because that statement cites only the published PCT and omits provisional page references. It remains material to the accuracy of the internal memorandum and to any later provisional-priority analysis.

**Interim control:** Do not replace the memorandum's provisional pinpoints with the page numbers stated in the email unless the underlying counsel copy and its pagination are identified. Any use of provisional citations must identify both the printed specification page and the page number of the particular PDF being cited.

**Required resolution:** Obtain from US counsel the exact provisional file or Patent Center document view used for the 30–31 and 44–45 references, reconcile it against `PPA2/as filed 63 557868.pdf`, and then state any parallel pagination explicitly. If counsel used the same local source, ask counsel to confirm corrected page references.
