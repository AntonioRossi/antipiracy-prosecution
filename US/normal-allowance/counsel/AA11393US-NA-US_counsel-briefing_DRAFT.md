<a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-para-00067"></a>
NA claim 15 directly recites:

1. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-orderedlist-00068"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00069"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00070"></a> a ledger associating delivered manifests with recipients;

3. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00073"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00074"></a> plural suspect camera-cut time-code detection;

6. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00079"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00080"></a> recipient identification.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-para-00081"></a>
A4 and A6 are strong on manifest delivery, suspect-pattern recovery, and recipient resolution. A13 is the closest stored manifest-regeneration reference but reconstructs from segment identifiers rather than scene-change-derived cut times. A21 detects shot boundaries, derives a shot-duration/time-slice fingerprint, and performs exact database fingerprint comparison; it does not reconstruct a delivered manifest or identify a recipient from a delivered-manifest ledger. B6 and B9 create recipient-specific timing pressure; B8 creates relative-time recovery pressure; A20 supplies forensic EDL/registry motivation; and A5 and B7 pressure perceptual-hash and shot-detection implementations. NA claim 15 anchors the ensemble on the delivered side to the per-cut ordered physical-camera transition without requiring suspect-side physical-camera identification. Its principal routes are A4+B9+A13+A21 or A5/B7, A6+A13+A21 or B6/B8, A20+A13+A21/B7 with B6/B9, and A4/B9+A13 with A21 or other cut detection as needed.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-na-us-counsel-briefing-para-00082"></a>
NA claim 18 requires the delivered manifest found equal by the search to select a mate chunk spanning a per-cut ordered transition at a noncoincident timing, including temporally corresponding different-camera frames in the intervening interval. The plural recipient timing combination is no longer a separate dependent: NA claim 15 recites the reconstructed manifest representing the detected combination and the equal manifest matching it. Test both against A20+B9+A4/A6/A13+A21 and direct multicamera/EDL art; the words `mate` and `manifest` are not, by themselves, distinctions.
