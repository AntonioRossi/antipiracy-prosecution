## 2. Allowance thesis

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00024"></a>
AF claims 1 and 17 require the following linked chain:

1. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-orderedlist-00025"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00026"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00027"></a> receive plural-camera video and a structured edit list containing, for plural director-commanded cuts, successive entries identifying different physical source cameras and paired out/in-point time codes;
2. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00028"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00029"></a> at plural selected cuts, modify those paired time codes while retaining the camera identifiers and order, producing reference and mate transition timings;
3. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00030"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00031"></a> segment the reference/mate ensemble, generate manifests pointing to respective chunk combinations, each preserving a per-cut choice between the reference and later mate timings of the same ordered transition, deliver according to those manifests, and associate each delivered manifest with its recipient in a ledger;
4. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00032"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00033"></a> detect a plurality of camera-cut time codes in suspected content and build one or more reconstructed manifests from those time codes, including one representing the detected combination of camera-switch timings; and
5. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-list-item-00034"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-plain-00035"></a> identify the recipient associated with a delivered manifest equal to the reconstructed manifest, the equal manifest's recipient-associated combination including at least one mate timing and matching the detected combination.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00036"></a>
Each output constrains a later operation. The structured list generates the reference and mates; the reference/mate ensemble supplies manifest-selected chunks; delivery populates the ledger; and plural detected cut times produce the reconstructed object used in the ledger search.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00037"></a>
The reviewed art densely teaches manifest delivery, temporal variants, scene-change or timing analysis, manifest reconstruction, recipient records, and tracing. No reviewed document was identified disclosing the complete claimed conjunction with plural recorded boundary changes between retained identified physical cameras. That limited-search result is not a clearance or allowance prediction.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00038"></a>
AF claim 17 provides a method-performance category, not a different technical novelty theory. AF claim 20 is a distinct monitor-side enforcement position: it omits affirmative production and delivery, and its patentability position rests on the per-cut ordered-transition delivered-manifest environment and the affirmative mate-containing delivered-combination-to-reconstructed-same-combination nexus.

## 6. Enforcement and evidence trade

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00176"></a>
AF claims 1 and 17–19 trade actor-specific breadth for a concentrated chain. AF claim 20 targets the actor performing the recovery method and does not require that actor to perform production, segmentation, delivery, or ledger recording.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-header-00177"></a>
### AF claim 1 — whole-system use

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00178"></a>
Counsel should analyze whether a target makes, sells, offers, or uses the claimed system as a whole. Under the fact-specific `Centillion` framework, a party may use a distributed system by putting the whole system into service, controlling that use, and obtaining its benefit. `CloudofChange` confirms that supplying or hosting part of a system does not necessarily make the vendor the user when customers initiate and benefit from the whole-system operation.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-header-00179"></a>
### AF claim 17 — method performance and attribution

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00180"></a>
Direct method infringement ordinarily requires every step to be performed by one entity or attributable to one entity. Under `Akamai`, attribution may arise through agency, contract, joint enterprise, or direction/control, including conditioning participation or a benefit on performance while establishing the manner or timing. A commercial relationship or platform alone is insufficient.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00181"></a>
Do not transpose the tests: method-step attribution does not itself establish whole-system use, and system use does not dispense with proof of every method step.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-header-00182"></a>
### AF claim 20 — monitor-side performance

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-counsel-briefing-para-00183"></a>

Ledger ownership or physical location is not dispositive; the operative questions are who performs the remote search-to-identify operation and whether another actor's conduct is legally attributable to the accused entity. A monitoring vendor that supplies cut times or a reconstructed object while a platform backend performs the search-to-identify operation does not necessarily perform the complete claim. Counsel must also determine the domestic location of every claimed method step.
