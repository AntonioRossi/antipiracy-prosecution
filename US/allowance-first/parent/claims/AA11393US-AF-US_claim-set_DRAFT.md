## 1. AF objective and architecture

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-para-00007"></a>
This branch contains **one independent system claim and one independent method claim that each recite the complete production-to-attribution chain with the timing-choice nexus built in, together with one monitor-side independent method claim directed to the disclosed recipient-recovery operation over a per-cut ordered-transition ensemble**. Its allowance-first premise is that the strongest present distinction is not any isolated use of segmentation, manifests, timing fingerprints, shot detection, or a recipient ledger. For AF claims 1 and 17, it is the operational combination in which:

1. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-orderedlist-00008"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-list-item-00009"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-plain-00010"></a> successive edit entries identify different physical source cameras and their in-point and out-point time codes;
2. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-list-item-00011"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-plain-00012"></a> recorded time codes at a plurality of director-commanded cuts are varied while the source-camera identifiers and their order are retained, producing reference and mate versions with noncoincident timings for the retained camera transitions;
3. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-list-item-00013"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-plain-00014"></a> manifest files direct delivery of respective combinations of reference and mate chunks, each preserving a per-cut choice between the two timings of the same ordered transition so that delivered manifests represent recipient-associated combinations of timing choices across the plural selected cuts, and are associated with recipients in a ledger; and
4. <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-list-item-00015"></a> <a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-plain-00016"></a> plural camera-cut time codes detected in suspected content are used to build a reconstructed manifest representing a detected combination of camera-switch timings across the plural selected cuts, and the ledger is searched for the recipient associated with an equal delivered manifest whose recipient-associated combination includes at least one mate timing and matches the detected combination.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-para-00017"></a>
AF claim 1 recites that integrated system, and AF claim 17 recites the same chain as affirmative method steps. The per-cut timing-choice nexus — manifest combinations preserving per-cut choices, reconstruction of the detected combination, and a match requiring an affirmative mate timing — is recited in the independent claims, not held as fallback; perceptual-hash and sliding-window detection, delivery variants, and probabilistic collusion tracing remain dependent implementation rungs. Each output in the integrated claims is used by a later operation: the edit instructions determine the reference and mates; those versions supply the chunks selected by delivered manifests; the delivered manifests populate the recipient ledger; and plural detected cut time codes produce the reconstructed-manifest object used for recipient identification.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-para-00018"></a>
AF claim 20 places suspect acquisition, plural cut-time derivation, reconstruction, and ledger search to identify a recipient in one affirmative monitor-side method. Its searched-ledger environment requires delivered reference/mate chunk-combination manifests in which, for each of the plurality of camera cuts, the reference contains an ordered transition between respective first and second source cameras and at least one mate contains the same ordered transition at a different timing; the equal delivered manifest must contain an affirmative mate timing and match the same detected plural timing combination represented by the reconstruction. It does not require the monitoring actor to produce, segment, deliver, or record the reference/mate versions and does not require suspect-side identification of physical camera sources.

<a id="ssp-aa11393us-af-us-claim-set-para-00019"></a>
The integrated claims trade enforcement breadth for a concentrated patentability position and may be best suited to a vertically integrated operator or a system whose components are operated or controlled by one entity. AF claim 20 targets a narrower direct-performer position but carries a materially more exposed recovery-side art, eligibility, support, restriction, and proof posture. The NA branch should remain available for production, distribution, detection-system, and broader method coverage, whether in the initial case or in properly supported continuation/divisional practice selected by counsel.

### Integrated allowance-first system

<a id="ssp-claim-1"></a>
**1.** A system for generating recipient-associated distinguishable versions of audio-video content and identifying a recipient associated with a suspected unauthorized distribution of the audio-video content, the system comprising one or more processors and memory storing instructions that, when executed by the one or more processors, cause the system to:
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-1"></a>

receive video captured from a plurality of cameras and a structured list of edit instructions comprising, for each of a plurality of director-commanded camera cuts, a first edit entry comprising an out-point time code and a first source-camera identifier identifying a first camera of the plurality of cameras as a first source camera, and a following edit entry comprising an in-point time code and a second source-camera identifier identifying a second camera of the plurality of cameras as a second source camera different from the first source camera;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-2"></a>

produce reference audio-video content according to the structured list of edit instructions;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-3"></a>

generate one or more mates of the reference audio-video content by, for each of a plurality of selected camera cuts among the director-commanded camera cuts, modifying, in the structured list of edit instructions, the out-point time code of the corresponding first edit entry and the in-point time code of the corresponding following edit entry while retaining the first and second source-camera identifiers and the order of the first and second source cameras, such that, for each selected camera cut:
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-4"></a>

a transition from the first source camera to the second source camera occurs at a reference camera-switch timing in the reference audio-video content and at a later mate camera-switch timing in at least one of the one or more mates; and
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-5"></a>

in the at least one mate, selection of the first source camera is extended from the reference camera-switch timing to the later mate camera-switch timing and selection of the second source camera begins at the later mate camera-switch timing;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-6"></a>

segment an ensemble comprising the reference audio-video content and the one or more mates into chunks;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-7"></a>

generate a plurality of manifest files pointing to respective combinations of chunks selected from the ensemble, each respective combination of chunks preserving, for each of the plurality of selected camera cuts, a choice between the reference camera-switch timing and the later mate camera-switch timing of that selected camera cut, so that the respective combinations of chunks represent respective combinations of timing choices across the plurality of selected camera cuts;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-8"></a>

cause delivery of streamed audio-video content to respective recipients according to respective manifest files of the plurality of manifest files;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-9"></a>

store, in a ledger, associations between the respective manifest files and the recipients to which the streamed audio-video content was delivered according to the respective manifest files;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-10"></a>

receive the suspected unauthorized distribution;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-11"></a>

apply a scene-change detection algorithm to the suspected unauthorized distribution to identify a plurality of time codes of camera cuts in the suspected unauthorized distribution;
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-12"></a>

build one or more reconstructed manifest files from the plurality of identified time codes, the one or more reconstructed manifest files including a reconstructed manifest file representing a detected combination of camera-switch timings across the plurality of selected camera cuts; and
<a id="ssp-claim-1-limitation-13"></a>

search the ledger to identify a recipient associated with a delivered manifest file that is equal to the reconstructed manifest file, the delivered manifest file that is equal to the reconstructed manifest file representing a recipient-associated combination of timing choices that includes at least one of the later mate camera-switch timings and matches the detected combination represented by the reconstructed manifest file.
